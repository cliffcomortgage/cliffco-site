How the program works

Three NJHMFA programs, combined into one.

The First Generation Homebuyer Program provides $7,000 on its own. Paired with NJHMFA's Smart Start Down Payment Assistance Program (up to $15,000, depending on county) and an NJHMFA first mortgage, qualified buyers get up to $22,000 total toward their down payment or closing costs.

Both assistance pieces are structured as 0% interest loans with no monthly payment, forgiven after 5 years of living in the home as your primary residence. Cliffco originates the first mortgage and helps you apply for both assistance programs as one process, not three separate applications.

Before you ask

The questions we hear most, answered directly.

My parents used to own a home. Doesn't that disqualify me?

Not necessarily. The rule is about present ownership, not history. If your parents don't currently own any residential real estate, anywhere, you can still qualify even if they owned a home in the past and sold it. A Cliffco loan officer can confirm your specific situation.

Is this actually free money?

It's a forgivable loan, not a grant: $0 interest, no monthly payment, and forgiven if you live in the home as your primary residence for 5 years without refinancing. If you sell, move out, or refinance before then, the assistance becomes repayable.

What if my county isn't one of the ones offering $22,000?

Every county in New Jersey qualifies for this program. Twelve counties (including Bergen, Essex, Middlesex, and Union) offer the full $22,000. The remaining nine offer $17,000. Either way, you're getting real assistance toward your purchase.

Do I need excellent credit to qualify?

You'll need to meet NJHMFA's credit score and debt-to-income guidelines, which are more flexible than many buyers assume. Checking your eligibility costs nothing and doesn't affect your credit.

Will I have to deal with multiple lenders or agencies?

No. Your Cliffco loan officer originates the NJHMFA first mortgage and handles the Down Payment Assistance and First Generation Homebuyer paperwork as one combined application.

Common questions

First Generation Homebuyer Program FAQ.

What exactly is the NJHMFA First Generation Homebuyer Program?

It's a New Jersey state program that provides $7,000 toward a down payment or closing costs, on top of NJHMFA's Smart Start Down Payment Assistance Program. Combined, qualified buyers can receive up to $22,000, depending on the county. It must be paired with an NJHMFA first mortgage.

Is it a loan or a grant?

It's structured as a forgivable loan: 0% interest, no monthly payments, and forgiven in full after 5 years of owner-occupancy without refinancing or otherwise conveying the first mortgage.

Who counts as a first-generation homebuyer?

A first-time homebuyer whose parents or legal guardians have no present ownership interest in any residential property anywhere, and whose spouse or domestic partner hasn't owned a primary residence in the past 3 years. It also includes anyone who was ever in foster care in New Jersey, was an emancipated youth, or was a homeless or unaccompanied youth under the McKinney-Vento Act.

How much assistance is available in my county?

Twelve counties qualify for $22,000 total: Bergen, Essex, Hudson, Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex, Monmouth, Morris, Ocean, Passaic, Somerset, Union. The remaining nine counties qualify for $17,000 total: Atlantic, Burlington, Camden, Cape May, Cumberland, Gloucester, Salem, Sussex, Warren.
