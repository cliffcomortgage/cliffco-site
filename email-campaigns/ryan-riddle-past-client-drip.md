## Email 3 — August 2026: Summer Renovations

**Subject:** The renovations that actually move the needle  
**Preview text:** Not all home improvements are worth the money.

---

[First Name],

Summer is renovation season. Before you spend, here's what I've seen consistently hold value vs. what doesn't:

**Worth it:** Kitchen updates (cosmetic, not gut), bathroom refreshes, curb appeal, new entry door, energy-efficient windows.

**Usually not worth it:** Pools (market-dependent), over-improving for the neighborhood, luxury finishes that don't match the street.

**Don't skip:** Roof, HVAC, foundation. They won't wow buyers, but deferred maintenance will kill your sale.

If you're doing something bigger and want to talk through financing options, there are products that make a lot more sense than putting it on a credit card.
