## Discrepancies / items to verify

- **AZ license number variance.** Most disclosures show AZ Mortgage Banker License **1045708**. Julian Giaquinto's disclosure (line 52 of CSV) shows AZ Mortgage Banker License **0949291**. Likely a stale or branch-specific number — verify with NMLS Consumer Access for Cliffco's current AZ Mortgage Banker license and reconcile.
- **States missing license numbers in disclosure text** (CO, MD, NJ, NM, TX) — license exists but the displayed disclosure text doesn't carry the number. For some states (NJ DOBI) the convention is to omit the number; for others it should be added for completeness. Verify on a state-by-state basis with compliance.
- **MA presence** — only Dick Lee shows MA in his license list, but corporate-wide compliance roster includes MA Mortgage Broker and Mortgage Lender License MC65328. Confirm MA is a corporate-active state vs. an LO-only state.
