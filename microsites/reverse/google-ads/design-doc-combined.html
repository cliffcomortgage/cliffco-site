Google Ads API Design Document

Prepared for: Google Ads API Access Review

1. Company Name

Cliffco Mortgage Bankers

2. Business Model

Cliffco Mortgage Bankers is a licensed mortgage lender offering home purchase loans, refinancing, and reverse mortgage products to consumers in the United States. All advertising is placed exclusively for Cliffco Mortgage Bankers' own products and services — no third-party clients are managed. The company owns and operates all websites and landing pages associated with its Google Ads campaigns.

3. Tool Access / Use

This tool is for internal use only. Access is restricted to marketing staff at Cliffco Mortgage Bankers. No external users, customers, or third parties have access. The tool is not offered as a service or product to anyone outside the organization. A single Google Ads Manager Account (MCC) is used to access the company's own advertising accounts.

4. Tool Design

The tool reads reporting data from the Google Ads API using GoogleAdsService.search and surfaces it in an internal dashboard. Data queried includes campaign performance metrics, keyword performance, Quality Score, search term reports, and ad group statistics. No data is stored in external systems or shared with third parties.

Write operations are user-initiated only. A staff member reviews suggested optimizations (keyword additions, negative keyword additions, bid adjustments) and explicitly approves each change before the tool executes a mutate call. No automated write operations occur without human approval. Changes are limited to the company's own Google Ads accounts under the MCC.

5. API Services Called

  • GoogleAdsService.search — campaign, ad group, keyword, and search-term reporting
  • KeywordPlanIdeaService — keyword research and volume estimates
  • CampaignService.mutate — user-approved campaign status and budget changes
  • SharedSetService / SharedCriterionService — managing negative keyword lists
