# Cliffco — Photo Prompt Library

Seven working ChatGPT prompts, each paired with the image it produced.

**Two ways to use them:**

1. **Steal it.** Copy a prompt below verbatim. Paste it into ChatGPT. Attach a
   professional photo of yourself. Add the line:
   > *"Use the person in the attached photo as the subject of this prompt — keep my face, hair, and build, but apply everything else."*
2. **Delegate it.** Paste a prompt into ChatGPT and tell it:
   > *"Use this as a template for the kind of detail and structure I want. Now write me a similar prompt for [your situation in plain English]. Then generate the image."*

ChatGPT will use the example as its quality benchmark and write a brand-aligned prompt for you.

## A few rules to keep you out of trouble

- **Don't ask the model to render the Cliffco logo or any text inside the image.** Add those in Canva or Photoshop afterward.
- **Don't ask for a specific named real person** ("a photo of Christopher Clifford"). It won't render them — and may produce off-brand averaging. Describe the *type* of person you want.
- **For Florida-targeted content**, do not bake "Swish Capital, Inc." into the image. The DBA disclosure belongs in caption/overlay text added after.
- **Iterate, don't rewrite.** Once you have an image you like ~80%, just say things like *"make the lighting warmer,"* *"swap the suit for business casual,"* *"shoot from a lower angle,"* *"make it vertical 9:16 for Instagram."*
