Frequently asked

Questions we answer every day.

What is a foreign national loan and who qualifies?

A foreign national loan allows non-US citizens and non-permanent residents to purchase or refinance US real estate without a Social Security number. Eligible borrowers include visa holders, international investors, and individuals who live and work abroad. Documentation requirements differ from standard loans — typically a foreign passport, visa (if applicable), and evidence of foreign income or assets. Team Chen specializes in these transactions and works with programs that accommodate borrowers from most countries.

Can I qualify for a DSCR loan if I'm an international investor?

Yes. DSCR loans are particularly well-suited for foreign national investors because qualification is based on the rental income of the US property, not the borrower's personal income or employment. You do not need US tax returns, a Social Security number, or domestic income documentation. The property's cash flow carries the loan.

What is a Non-QM loan and how does it differ from a conventional mortgage?

Non-QM stands for Non-Qualified Mortgage — it doesn't conform to the standard Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines used by most banks. Non-QM loans use alternative documentation methods such as bank statements, P&L statements, asset depletion, or rental income to qualify borrowers. They're designed for self-employed professionals, investors, and borrowers with complex income structures. Team Chen originates Non-QM programs across all income documentation types.

What is a Business Bank Statement loan?

A business bank statement loan allows business owners to qualify using 12 to 24 months of business bank deposits rather than tax returns. A standard expense ratio is applied to the gross deposits to calculate qualifying income. This is designed for LLCs, S-Corps, and sole proprietors whose tax returns understate their actual cash flow. No W-2 or personal income documentation is required.
