Why DSCR + STR

The loan built for how STR cash flow actually works.

Traditional mortgage underwriting uses your W-2 or tax returns to determine what you can borrow. For STR investors — especially those using the STR tax loophole — those numbers often understate your actual financial position. DSCR loans solve this.

With a DSCR loan, the lender underwrites the property's income, not yours. If the projected or actual rental income covers the monthly debt service, the deal qualifies. No W-2. No tax returns. No personal income documentation.

This makes DSCR the ideal tool for high-income W-2 professionals whose tax returns look light due to depreciation — exactly the situation STR Like the Best clients are in.

STR investor questions

Common questions about STR financing.

Can I use Airbnb or VRBO income to qualify for a DSCR loan?

Yes. Cliffco's DSCR programs for short-term rentals evaluate the property's projected or actual short-term rental income — from AirDNA projections, a comparable market analysis, or your existing booking history. You do not need to show W-2 income or tax returns. The property's cash flow is what qualifies.

What DSCR ratio do I need for an STR property?

Most DSCR programs require a minimum ratio of 1.0 (rent covers the full PITIA), with best pricing at 1.25 or higher. Well-located short-term rentals in high-demand markets frequently achieve ratios well above 1.25, which is part of what makes the STR model attractive for DSCR financing.

Can I hold the property in an LLC?

Yes. Cliffco's DSCR programs allow LLC vesting, which is important for STR investors who want liability separation between their properties and personal assets. We can also fund out-of-state borrowers — if you're buying in a different state from where you live, that's no problem.

How much do I need to put down?

Most DSCR programs for investment properties require 20 to 25% down. Some programs go as low as 15% for strong credit profiles with solid projected DSCR ratios. Your Cliffco loan officer will show you the full range of options for your specific market and property.

Do I need to be a first-time real estate investor?

Not at all. DSCR loans work for first-time investors and experienced operators alike. Whether this is your first STR purchase or you're adding to an existing portfolio, the qualification is based on the property's income — not how many properties you currently own.

What's the process from first call to closing?

It starts with a short conversation about your target property and financial profile. Once we have basic info, we can issue a pre-approval letter typically within 24 hours. From there: appraisal (which includes a rental income analysis for STR properties), underwriting, and closing. DSCR loans typically close in 21 to 35 days.
