Frequently asked

Questions we answer every day.

What types of borrowers does The Fallarino Group work with?

We specialize in high-income professionals, entrepreneurs, business owners, and real estate investors whose financial profiles don't fit conventional bank guidelines. If you have complex income, need jumbo or super-jumbo financing, want to qualify on investment property cash flow, or are optimizing a tax strategy that makes your returns look light, we're built for exactly that.

What is the largest loan The Fallarino Group has originated?

Our programs reach into the super-jumbo range, up to $40M, on Alt-A and portfolio products. Loan size is rarely the limiting factor for our borrowers. The conversation starts with your financial profile and goals, not a loan limit.

How is Alt-A different from Non-QM?

Alt-A loans sit between A-paper conventional and Non-QM. They use the same tax returns as conventional underwriting but evaluate gross income and essential expenses rather than the after-deduction bottom line. This typically produces better rates than Non-QM while still accommodating the income complexity that makes conventional underwriting fall short for entrepreneurs.

Can I qualify for a DSCR loan if the property is an Airbnb or short-term rental?

Yes. DSCR programs accept projected or historical short-term rental income using AirDNA projections or your actual booking history. You do not need to show W-2 income. The property's cash flow is the qualification. LLC vesting is also allowed.

How quickly can The Fallarino Group issue a pre-approval?

In most cases, same day. Our team reviews complex financial files daily and understands how to move quickly without sacrificing precision. A pre-approval letter for a high-value purchase or investment transaction is typically issued within 24 hours of receiving your documentation.

Do you work with out-of-state buyers?

Yes. David is licensed in 13 states and the team collectively covers a broad footprint. If you're purchasing investment property outside New York or refinancing a property in another state, reach out and we'll confirm coverage.
