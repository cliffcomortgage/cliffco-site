About Gary

In their own words.

Gary Lai is a seasoned mortgage loan officer with Cliffco Mortgage Bankers, bringing over 20 years of lending experience to clients across New York City and Northern New Jersey. Starting his career in 2003 as a loan assistant, Gary steadily grew into a top-producing role, known for providing personalized mortgage solutions that meet the needs of today's diverse borrowers. He works with a wide range of clients, from first-time homebuyers to experienced real estate investors, offering financing tailored to conventional, jumbo, condo, and co-op properties. Gary is especially skilled at guiding self-employed borrowers through flexible programs such as bank statement, P&L, and alternative documentation loans. In addition to serving his clients, Gary has built strong partnerships with leading real estate teams throughout NYC and Northern NJ. He has also served as the AREAA National Mortgage Chair and AREAA National Secretary, reflecting his ongoing commitment to expanding access to homeownership in the community.

Common questions

Answers before you reach out.

How do I get started?

The first step is a short conversation — no credit pull, no paperwork. Gary will ask about your goals, income situation, and timeline to identify the right program. From there, you can move into a formal pre-approval when you're ready.

Will getting pre-qualified hurt my credit score?

Pre-qualification at Cliffco uses a soft credit inquiry, which has no impact on your score. A hard pull only happens when you formally apply for a loan and authorize it — and we'll always tell you before that step.

What's the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?

Pre-qualification is a quick estimate based on what you share with us. Pre-approval goes deeper — we verify your income, assets, and credit and issue a formal letter. Sellers take pre-approved buyers more seriously, and in competitive markets it's often required before an offer is considered.

How long does the mortgage process take?

A conventional purchase typically closes in 21–45 days from an accepted offer. Non-QM, DSCR, and reverse mortgage transactions can take 30–45 days. Your timeline depends on the loan type, appraisal scheduling, and how quickly documents come together — we'll give you a realistic estimate at the start.

What documents will I need?

For most loans: two years of tax returns, recent pay stubs or bank statements, W-2s, and two months of asset statements. For non-QM and bank statement loans, requirements differ — typically 12–24 months of personal or business bank statements in place of tax returns. We'll give you a specific checklist based on your situation.

Can I qualify if I'm self-employed?

Yes. Cliffco specializes in non-traditional income scenarios — bank statement loans, P&L-based loans, DSCR for investors, and Alt-A programs for high-income borrowers whose tax returns don't reflect their full financial picture. Being self-employed isn't a barrier; it just means using the right program.
